The Compliance Reality

BRSR is not a form.
It is a data collection
operation across your
entire organisation.

01 Finance & Accounts GHG emissions, energy consumption, water withdrawal, waste generated — pulled from utility bills, P&L statements, vendor invoices. Typically 6–8 weeks to collect.
02 Human Resources Headcount, diversity ratios, training hours, safety incidents, contractor data across all sites. Spread across 4+ systems.
03 Legal & Secretarial Board composition, whistleblower policy, litigation disclosures, CSR spending, governance certifications. Manual extraction from board minutes.
04 Supply Chain & Procurement Supplier ESG assessments, responsible sourcing, conflict minerals, vendor certifications. Often not collected at all.
